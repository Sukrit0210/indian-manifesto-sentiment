# English edition checks

The source registry contains one row per requested party and election. The CSV records the document URL, exact SHA-256 fingerprint, inclusion status and extracted counts. Four elections were in scope for BJP, INC, CPI(M) and AIADMK. TMC and DMK enter in 2014 by design.

- Digital Sansad lists an item labelled “2024(English)” under All India Anna Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam in its state-party catalogue. Page 3 of the linked 68-page file identifies Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam. Its SHA-256 fingerprint is `c002990c4ed26d42669e9f860efbe4503a9a68d6cc5ed3dcd6eb99ecc4fa39c8`, an exact match to the registered DMK 2024 source. The AIADMK 2009, 2014 and 2019 parliamentary PDFs carry the correct party name. [Digital Sansad catalogue](https://sansad.in/ls/library/manifestos) · [File under the 2024 AIADMK entry](https://sansad.in/uploads/DMK_Election_Manifesto_Eng_Karthi_33ac415568_ad861eddfe.pdf)
- The Audit India Manifesto Library has archived BJP and Congress Lok Sabha material. Its catalogue yielded no AIADMK 2024 parliamentary manifesto. [Audit India](https://www.auditindia.org/en/manifesto-library/)
- The AIADMK party website presents its 2024 parliamentary manifesto in Tamil. The corpus requires an existing full English edition. AIADMK 2024 has missing status for both projects. [Party release](https://aiadmk.com/manifesto2024/)
- The English TMC manifesto under the party archive’s Lok Sabha 2014 entry is 14 pages. The supplied 50-page file has “Kolkata Municipal Election 2015” on its cover. [Official Lok Sabha PDF](https://static.aitcofficial.org/uploads/2014/10/Manifesto-LS-2014-Eng.pdf)
- DMK 2014 was recovered from The Hindu Centre’s archive. The PDF cover says “Parliamentary Elections 2014” and “The D.M.K. Manifesto.” DMK 2019 is also an English parliamentary scan. [DMK 2014 archive entry](https://www.thehinducentre.com/resources/dmk-manifesto-for-lok-sabha-2014/article64936389.ece)
